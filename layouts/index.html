WHY THE FUND MATTERS

Great research begins with the opportunity to do it.

Across Africa, talented master’s and PhD students are working to answer urgent agricultural questions. Too often, a shortage of research funding limits the experiments they can undertake, the evidence they can generate and the findings they can share.

The African Agricultural Scholars Research Fund is being developed to help close that gap—connecting eligible postgraduate scholars with research support, experienced mentors and a wider community of agricultural scientists.

OUR COMMITMENT

Research capacity is an investment in Africa’s future.

By supporting scholars and the research they lead, we aim to strengthen the evidence base for better decisions, more resilient agriculture and improved livelihoods across the continent.

HOW WE AIM TO HELP
