## Working together for stronger agricultural research

The African Agricultural Scholars Research Fund seeks to work with organisations and individuals who believe that supporting African postgraduate researchers is an investment in the continent’s future.

### Who we hope to work with

- **Universities and postgraduate schools** interested in widening research opportunities for their students.
- **Research institutions and scientific networks** able to contribute expertise, facilities and mentorship.
- **Foundations and development partners** seeking to support agricultural research and capacity development.
- **Private-sector organisations** interested in evidence, innovation and sustainable agricultural development.
- **Alumni associations and individuals** who want to help emerging scholars undertake meaningful research.

### Ways to contribute

Potential contributions may include financial support, research equipment and materials, laboratory access, technical expertise, mentorship, training opportunities and support for research dissemination.

### Partnership principles

We aim to build relationships based on shared objectives, transparency, responsible resource management, scientific independence and equitable opportunity.

The Fund is currently developing its institutional and operational arrangements. Partnership discussions are welcome; formal partnership opportunities and contribution mechanisms will be published when established.
