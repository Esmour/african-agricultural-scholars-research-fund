## Updates from the Fund

This page will share announcements about the Fund’s development, future calls for proposals, mentorship activities, partnerships and research stories from supported scholars.

**No funding call is currently open.** Official opportunities will be published here when they are confirmed.

### Useful research resources

While the Fund’s programmes are being developed, postgraduate researchers may find the following resources useful:

- [ORCID](https://orcid.org/) — a persistent digital identifier for researchers.
- [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/) — discover scholarly literature and track research profiles.
- [FAO](https://www.fao.org/) — global knowledge and resources on food and agriculture.
- [CGIAR](https://www.cgiar.org/) — international agricultural research and innovation.
- [African Union](https://au.int/) — continental policy, programmes and institutions.

If you have a research opportunity, resource or story relevant to African agricultural scholars, please contact us.
