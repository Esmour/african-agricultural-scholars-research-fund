## Supporting scholars beyond the grant

The Fund recognises that postgraduate researchers benefit from access to scientific advice, constructive feedback and professional networks. Mentorship is therefore an important part of the initiative’s proposed model.

### Areas of support

- **Research design:** refining research questions, hypotheses, experimental design and sampling strategies.
- **Data analysis:** selecting appropriate analytical approaches, interpreting results and improving reproducibility.
- **Scientific communication:** developing manuscripts, conference presentations, policy briefs and accessible summaries.
- **Research planning:** managing timelines, field activities, budgets and research milestones.
- **Professional development:** building networks, exploring collaboration and preparing for research careers.

### A collaborative model

Mentorship is intended to be matched to the needs and research area of participating scholars, subject to mentor availability and programme arrangements. The Fund will seek to build connections among African universities, research institutions and experienced scientists across the continent and internationally.

### Interested in becoming a mentor?

Experienced researchers interested in contributing time, advice or specialist expertise are invited to contact the initiative.
